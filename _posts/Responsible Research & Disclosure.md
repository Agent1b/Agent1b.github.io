# Responsible Research & Disclosure

I conduct security research only on systems I own, isolated lab environments, public patched CVEs, or systems where I have explicit authorization.

I do not perform unauthorized testing, credential theft, data exfiltration, malware deployment, persistence on third-party systems, or activity outside an approved scope.

For active vendor reports, I follow coordinated vulnerability disclosure and withhold technical details until the vendor has provided guidance or a fix is available.

My public work is intended for defensive education: vulnerability understanding, remediation, IOCs, Sigma rules, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, and blue-team detection improvement.
